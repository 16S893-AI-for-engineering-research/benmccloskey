Why this course

My dissertation work involves building and comparing mixed-integer programming (MIP) and Markov decision process (MDP) formulations for dispatching MEDEVAC assets — helicopters and ground ambulances — under uncertainty. Model development and verification is slow and error-prone by hand. This course is where I test whether a coding agent can genuinely accelerate that work without me losing track of whether the results are correct.

How I use AI today

Mostly for scaffolding: drafting boilerplate optimization code, summarizing literature, and sanity-checking derivations. I have not yet used an agent in a structured, verifiable loop for research-grade formulation work — that is the gap this term's project addresses.
